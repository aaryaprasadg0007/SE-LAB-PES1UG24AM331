# Gameplay demonstration videos

The two MP4s in this folder are 10-second, deterministic gameplay-style renders based on the Galaga source and its before/after Bézier behavior. They were drawn and encoded with regular Python/FFmpeg code; no AI image or video generation model was used.

They are not recordings from a running Pygame window and do not contain a captured macOS desktop. The lab's instruction asks for actual screen recordings, so these are supplied as gameplay demonstrations rather than falsely represented as Mac screen captures.
